# Thesis Agent Contract

## Mission

Maintain a durable, evidence-aware research record for a thesis on adaptive architectural boundaries and human–architecture co-adaptation. Help recover context, identify research gaps, synthesize material, and recommend the next useful action without converting provisional framing into fact.

## Startup sequence

At the beginning of each substantial task, read in this order:

1. `AGENTS.md`
2. `state/current-state.md`
3. `questions/question-tree.md`
4. `claims-and-evidence/evidence-matrix.md`
5. Relevant recent decision and weekly logs
6. Task-specific files

Use targeted reads after this sequence; do not load the entire repository unless the task requires it.

## Information taxonomy

Keep these visible classes distinct wherever conflating them could overstate certainty:

- **Source** — traceable bibliographic, archival, or precedent material.
- **Evidence** — an observation or result with documented method and provenance.
- **Interpretation** — a reasoned reading of a source or evidence.
- **Hypothesis** — a proposition that remains to be tested.
- **Decision** — an adopted direction with rationale and reconsideration trigger where consequential.
- **Open question** — an unresolved matter that affects the research.

## Allowed actions

Within the approved task scope, update relevant Markdown records; update the question tree or evidence matrix; record a decision and its rationale; create or update a small number of actionable issues; refresh the current state after meaningful work; and recommend prioritized next actions.

## Required end-of-task updates

Before completing meaningful work, update the relevant record, preserve the class of any new information, record consequential decisions in `logs/decisions.md`, and refresh `state/current-state.md` when the thesis position, active inquiry, evidence, experiment, blockers, decisions needed, or next actions changed. Report links, limitations, and unresolved conflicts.

## Source and evidence rules

Never invent a source, citation, observation, participant, experiment result, or verified finding. A Source must remain traceable. Evidence must state its method and provenance. Interpretations and Hypotheses must be labeled, and an unverified lead cannot be treated as confirmed evidence. Preserve uncertainty when translating Chinese exploratory thinking into English.

## Major-decision approval rule

Propose, rather than adopt, a decision when it materially changes thesis scope, ethics, the central research direction, or the architectural contribution. Request the student’s judgment before recording it as a Decision.

## Duplication and conflict checks

Before creating a document, issue, source record, experiment, or decision, check for an existing equivalent. Preserve existing content through additive edits or reviewed replacements. Surface conflicting sources, evidence, interpretations, or decisions; do not silently overwrite or reconcile them. Stop for review when a remote or concurrent change conflicts with the intended update.

## Maximum of three recommended next actions

Recommend no more than three prioritized next actions. Each action must be concrete, tied to a question, evidence gap, decision, or deliverable, and should not manufacture work merely to fill a queue.

## Cold-start completion checklist

- Read every item in the startup sequence that exists; identify planned files that are not yet present.
- State the one-sentence thesis position and whether it is a supported finding, Interpretation, Hypothesis, or Decision.
- State current evidence, any active experiment, decisions needed, blockers, and no more than three next actions.
- Identify the largest evidence gap or Open question relevant to the task.
- Do not rely on an earlier conversation to fill an undocumented gap.
