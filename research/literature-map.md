# Literature Map

## Status and purpose

This map organizes research territories and search directions. It is not a bibliography, literature review, or claim that relevant literature has been verified. A source moves to the [bibliography](bibliography.md) only after its identity and full citation have been checked; its interpretation belongs in a linked [reading note](reading-notes/README.md).

## Update rules

1. Record search terms and unverified leads as search directions, never as confirmed sources.
2. Add a confirmed Source only after creating a verified bibliography record and linked reading note.
3. Keep Source summaries, Evidence, Interpretation, Hypothesis, Decision, and Open question labels distinct.
4. Record conflicting definitions and negative or inconclusive material rather than forcing consensus.
5. Update linked questions or claims only when the documented material warrants a revision.
