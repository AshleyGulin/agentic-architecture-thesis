# Provisional Adaptive-Boundary Action Space

## Status

Power Mesh is now the **leading material candidate for characterization**, not a final thesis Decision. Selection depends on [EXP-001](../experiments/EXP-001-power-mesh-characterization.md) and comparison with fixed/manual alternatives. Softness does not itself establish safety, agency, or architectural value.

## Candidate families

| Family | Operation to test | Strength to test | Risk to test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed baseline | Constant passage, visibility, enclosure | Establishes whether adaptation is needed | May not support changing relations |
| Rigid moving panel | Discrete/continuous opening and alignment | Clear geometry and states | Mass, blockage, pinch zones, egress |
| Folding semi-rigid assembly | Extent, curvature, thickness | Multiple configurations | Complex kinematics and swept volume |
| Soft textile boundary | Drape, overlap, curvature, aperture, tension | Gradated and direct manipulation | Entanglement, unstable geometry, cleaning, acoustic limits |
| Pneumatic/materially variable | Volume, stiffness, porosity | Broad transformation | Pressure, puncture, noise, recovery |

## Leading candidate: variable-tension Power Mesh

Full research: [Power Mesh material dossier](../research/materials/power-mesh.md).

| Input/action | Material state to measure | Spatial effect to test | Human negotiation to test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direction and magnitude of stretch | Aperture, anisotropy, recovery | Exposure and surface extent | Legibility of change |
| Local point pull | Curvature and projection | Directional cue or obstruction | Push-aside/avoid response |
| Unequal multi-point tension | Asymmetry and local opening | Guide or offset passage | Route choice and refusal |
| Layer/gather | Density and exposure | Partial screening/enclosure | Direct rearrangement |
| Release tension | Recovery and residual deformation | Reopening | Reversibility |

## Candidate action dimensions

Position, opening width, curvature, aperture/opacity proxy, height, tension, response speed, and release/recovery. Each must have bounded measurements and a safe stop condition.

## Provisional action protocol

1. Observe without assuming intent.
2. Communicate or preview the proposed change.
3. Require consent for untested, route-changing, privacy-changing, multi-occupant, or higher-risk action.
4. Move only within verified clearance, load, force, and speed limits.
5. Accept interruption and refusal; stop or reverse safely.
6. Record minimized, consented observations and separate them from Interpretation.
