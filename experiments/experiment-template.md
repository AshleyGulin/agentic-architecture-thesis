## Preregistered success/failure criteria

- **Success criterion:** Define before the run; include measure, threshold or qualitative rule, comparison, and required data quality.
- **Failure/disconfirmation criterion:** Define before the run; include unsafe, coercive, null, contradictory, or unusable outcomes where relevant.
- **Inconclusive criterion:** Define missingness, protocol deviation, insufficient contrast, or ambiguity that prevents a Result.
- **Stopping criterion:** Define safety, distress, consent withdrawal, obstruction, equipment, and data-quality triggers.
