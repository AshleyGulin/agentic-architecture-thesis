# EXP-002 — Variable-Tension Boundary Pilot

## Status

**Proposed follow-on study; dependent on EXP-001. Not approved or run.**

## Purpose

Test whether three bounded Power Mesh configurations—Open, Guide, and Enclose—are spatially legible, reversible, and physically rejectable before introducing autonomous sensing or actuation.

## Related inquiry

[Q2](../questions/question-tree.md#q2), [Q3](../questions/question-tree.md#q3), [Q4](../questions/question-tree.md#q4), and [Q5](../questions/question-tree.md#q5); [C2–C5 and C8–C9](../claims-and-evidence/evidence-matrix.md).

## Prototype

A roughly 50 × 70 cm Power Mesh panel on a stable frame, with up to 12 adjustable anchor points. Final dimensions and force limits must follow EXP-001.

## Conditions

- Fixed baseline.
- Direct manual configuration.
- Researcher-controlled Wizard-of-Oz proposal.
- No autonomous mode in the initial pilot.

## Proposed action sequence

Observe → propose a small change → allow refusal → move within limits → stop/reverse on override → record response without inferring intent.

## Measures to preregister

Opening geometry, path curvature, clearance, speed change, pause duration, touch/push/avoid actions, successful override, configuration recovery, participant-reported legibility and sense of control, plus failures and ambiguous cases.

## Ethics and safety gate

No participant recruitment or recording until the protocol, consent, privacy, egress, entanglement, fixture, load, emergency-stop, and data-retention procedures are approved by the relevant studio/institutional process.

## Raw Evidence and Results

None.

## Dependency

Proceed only if EXP-001 establishes bounded, repeatable material behavior and safe fixture conditions.
