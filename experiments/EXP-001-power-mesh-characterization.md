# EXP-001 — Power Mesh Characterization

## Status

**Planned desk/bench study; not yet run. No Results or Evidence exist.**

## Purpose

Determine whether a specific purchased Power Mesh sample offers stable, measurable relationships among direction of stretch, extension, layering, anchor configuration, aperture, curvature, visual exposure, and recovery.

## Related inquiry

[Q3](../questions/question-tree.md#q3); [C3](../claims-and-evidence/evidence-matrix.md#c3), [C6](../claims-and-evidence/evidence-matrix.md#c6), and [C7](../claims-and-evidence/evidence-matrix.md#c7).

## Hypotheses

- H1: direction, position, and magnitude of tension produce repeatable spatial differences.
- H2: the sample offers continuous rather than only binary material states.

## Materials and controls

- At least six 20 × 20 cm samples from one documented batch.
- 5 × 5 cm marked grid.
- Fixed background, illumination, camera position, lens, and viewing distance.
- Recorded fibre-content label, mass/area if available, cut orientation, fixing, and ambient conditions.
- Change one independent variable at a time and repeat each condition at least three times.

## Test series

A. Horizontal, vertical, and diagonal stretch.  
B. 20 cm gauge length extended to 20, 25, 30, and 35 cm, subject to a conservative material limit.  
C. One layer, aligned double layer, 45° double layer, triple local overlap, and gathering.  
D. Edge, point, linear-row, unequal multi-point, and opposing-point anchoring.

## Planned observations

Input displacement or load; aperture dimensions; grid deformation; curvature; transmitted-light proxy; edge curl; slip; damage; time and residual deformation after release. Photographs document rather than replace measurements.

## Safety and stop rules

No human participant exposure. Stop on tearing, permanent visible damage, fixture instability, unexpected snap-back, or any load beyond the safe capacity of the frame and fixings.

## Raw Evidence location

To be created under `experiments/data/EXP-001/` only after testing begins.

A calibrated comparison table, repeatability notes, failure log, and a justified go/no-go recommendation for EXP-002.
