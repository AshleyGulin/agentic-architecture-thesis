# Provisional Argument Outline

This is a claim -> Evidence needed -> possible design implication skeleton. It is not final chapter prose, and it does not imply that results exist. The current evidence for every seeded claim is **Not yet documented** in the [evidence matrix](../claims-and-evidence/evidence-matrix.md).

## Framing sequence

### A1 - Changing relations may create a need for adaptation

- **Claim or hypothesis:** **[C1](../claims-and-evidence/evidence-matrix.md#c1)** proposes that fixed boundaries may be insufficient in some changing domestic relations.
- **Related question:** [Q1 - Need](../questions/question-tree.md#q1).
- **Evidence needed:** Scenario-specific documentation of changing relations, comparison with fixed and manual arrangements, and disconfirming cases where adaptation adds no value.
- **Limitations to preserve:** The domestic context, relevant occupants, and baseline are not yet established.
- **Possible design implication:** If supported, the boundary action space should address a defined relational change rather than movement for its own sake. If unsupported, reduce or remove autonomous adaptation.

### A2 - Trajectory may offer an uncertain signal of spatial negotiation

- **Claim or hypothesis:** **[C2](../claims-and-evidence/evidence-matrix.md#c2)** proposes trajectory as a candidate signal when expanded to movement, distance, orientation, hesitation, dwell, and route choice.
- **Related question:** [Q2 - Perception](../questions/question-tree.md#q2).
- **Evidence needed:** Operational definitions, ethical observation or simulation, temporal comparisons, counterexamples, and explicit uncertainty records.
- **Limitations to preserve:** Behavior does not directly reveal intent, and sensor selection would introduce further limits.
- **Possible design implication:** If useful, the agent should respond to patterns with calibrated uncertainty and opportunities for correction. If unreliable, it should defer, request input, or not act.

### A3 - Gradated boundary actions may support negotiation

- **Claim or hypothesis:** **[C3](../claims-and-evidence/evidence-matrix.md#c3)** proposes that soft or continuously adjustable boundaries may enable more negotiable interventions than binary fixed partitions.
- **Related question:** [Q3 - Action space](../questions/question-tree.md#q3).
- **Evidence needed:** Comparative studies of candidate mechanisms, affordance changes, reversibility, safety, occupant response, and failure modes.
- **Limitations to preserve:** Softness is not equivalent to agency, and a curtain is only a candidate.
- **Possible design implication:** If supported, use a limited vocabulary of reversible, legible, gradated actions. If unsupported, compare other adaptive boundaries or retain manual control.

## Critical qualification

### A4 - Adaptation can become over-control

- **Claim or risk hypothesis:** **[C4](../claims-and-evidence/evidence-matrix.md#c4)** identifies over-control when inferred intent becomes authoritative or refusal and recovery are constrained.
- **Related question:** [Q4 - Human negotiation](../questions/question-tree.md#q4).
- **Evidence needed:** Documented cases of acceptance, interruption, refusal, reversal, appropriation, conflict, and recovery over repeated interactions.
- **Limitations to preserve:** Compliance is not sufficient evidence of voluntary acceptance, and non-coercion is not yet operationalized.
- **Possible design implication:** Treat interruption, reversal, and reinterpretation as feedback; expose uncertainty; keep manual and non-action pathways available.

## Intended contribution

### A5 - The contribution must be architectural, not merely mechanical

- **Claim or contribution hypothesis:** **[C5](../claims-and-evidence/evidence-matrix.md#c5)** proposes that contribution rests on changing spatial relations and preserving occupant agency rather than on movement alone.
- **Related question:** [Q5 - Architectural evaluation](../questions/question-tree.md#q5).
- **Evidence needed:** Explicit architectural criteria and documented comparison of spatial, experiential, and agency outcomes across adaptive, manual, and fixed conditions.
- **Limitations to preserve:** No evaluation criteria, comparison protocol, or results are yet documented.
- **Possible design implication:** Evaluate how the system changes passage, visibility, enclosure, proximity, connection, perceived choice, and appropriation over time; revise the design if technical performance lacks architectural consequence.

## Provisional synthesis

If future evidence supports A1 through A3 while surviving the qualification in A4, A5 could frame an architectural contribution around negotiated spatial change. If evidence challenges any step, the linked question and claim should be revised. No part of this sequence currently constitutes a supported finding.
