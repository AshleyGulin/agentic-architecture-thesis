## Use rules

- Add Evidence only with method, provenance, and limits.
- Keep Source, Evidence, Interpretation, Hypothesis, Decision, and Open question distinct.
- A mechanism or attractive material state is not Evidence of architectural value.
- Preserve stable IDs and log substantive revisions.
