## Use and update rules

- Add Evidence only when its method, provenance, and limits are documented.
- Keep Source, Evidence, Interpretation, Hypothesis, Decision, and Open question labels distinct.
- A completed design document or mechanism is not by itself evidence for a thesis claim.
- When a claim changes, retain its stable ID and document the revision rather than silently converting a Hypothesis into a finding.
- Follow the linked [question tree](../questions/question-tree.md) and [provisional argument outline](../writing/argument-outline.md) for the inquiry and argument context.
